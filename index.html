01 Systems

SYS — 01

M2 Giant Group Operations & Asset Management System

group project · 90% final mark

A business management application built for a real-world tree felling and vegetation management company, developed collaboratively in C# WinForms against a SQL Server backend. Covers the full operational loop: client intake, job requests, scheduling and calendar assignment, staff and asset allocation, and site photo documentation with before/after tagging.

  • Quote generation using Haversine-distance travel-fee calculation and 15% VAT
  • Invoicing and income/expense reporting via Crystal Reports
  • Site photo gallery with per-thumbnail error isolation
  • South African phone/email validation and coordinate bounding-box checks
  • Leaflet map embedded via WebView2 for job-site locations

SYS — 02

Fandom Factory — Multi-threaded Merchandise Store System

team of 7 · 94% final mark

A concurrent inventory and order-processing system written in C++, simulating simultaneous merchandise purchases without corrupting shared state. Synchronisation was handled with mutexes and smart pointers to prevent race conditions, overselling, and inconsistent stock during simultaneous transactions, built on a thread-safe, memory-safe object-oriented architecture.
